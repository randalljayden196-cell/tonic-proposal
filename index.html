01 / Understanding the Opportunity

Bringing the website up to the level of the company behind it.

After our discussion, the main opportunity appears to be less about completely reinventing the business, and more about presenting it the way it deserves to be presented.

Tonic already has strong foundations: established ERP expertise, clear industry experience, and reputable platform partnerships. The current opportunity is presenting all of that in a more modern, confidence-building, and conversion-focused way.

The goal of this project would be to create a cleaner and more modern web experience that positions Tonic more competitively, improves buyer confidence earlier, communicates value more clearly, creates a stronger first impression, and supports more conversations from interested prospects.

  • Established ERP expertise
  • Clear industry experience
  • Reputable platform partnerships

03 / Key Areas For Improvement

Four levers, working together.

Modernisation of the Overall UI

Refreshing the visual experience so the site feels current, polished, and aligned with modern B2B expectations, the kind of first impression that matches Tonic's actual capability.

Stronger Positioning & Messaging

Improving how the site communicates who Tonic is best for, why companies should choose Tonic specifically, and the value of working with an implementation partner, not just a platform.

Increased Trust & Reassurance

Introducing stronger proof and reassurance throughout the experience, built specifically for high-consideration buyers evaluating ERP partners over weeks, not minutes.

Improved Conversion Flow

Making the path from interest → enquiry feel natural and lower-friction for buyers who are still researching their options.

04 / Proposed Scope

Modernising the core, refreshing the rest.

Modernisation of core high-impact pages plus refreshed styling and layout alignment across the wider 19-page Squarespace site.

This project is not scoped as 19 individually custom-designed pages. The focus is redesigning the highest-impact pages, creating a cleaner visual system, and applying that system consistently across the wider site so the whole experience feels current, coherent, and conversion-focused.
